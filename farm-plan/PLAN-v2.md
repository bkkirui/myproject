# Farm Autonomy Plan — Draft v2 (for owner approval)

Date: 2026-10-15. Supersedes v1 (kept as PLAN-v1.md). Farm: cattle, sheep, goats, poultry, crops. Owner: busy academic; location unknown.

**Status of numbers.** Every number below is a **default to be calibrated** at Gate 0/1 with the named professional. None is a veterinary, agronomic or legal instruction. AI agents draft and tabulate; licensed humans decide (Section 9).

## 1. Principles (changed from v1)
1. **Management by exception, not by absence.** The owner stays out of daily work but keeps independent eyes on money, stock and data (Section 5).
2. **Evidence-gated autonomy.** Autonomy rises by gates with measurable exit criteria, never by calendar. There is no "hands-off at month 6".
3. **Economics and scope before SOPs.** No enterprise SOP is written until Gate 1 says the enterprise is viable, staffable and legal.
4. **Safety is never blocked on the owner.** Disease reporting, welfare and water/power emergencies are pre-authorised and need no owner reply.
5. **Any single person can be wrong, absent or dishonest**, including the manager, the owner and the auditor. Design for that.

## 2. Gate 0 — hard intake (no design work until answered)
Owner answers, plus a physical **baseline site audit** by a local professional (extension officer, consultant vet or farm consultant), weeks 1–3.

| Area | Questions |
|---|---|
| Place | Country/region, coordinates, hemisphere, rainfall regime, nearest vet and abattoir |
| Scale | Head counts by class, hectares by soil/use, poultry type and numbers, current output and **records/accounts for the last 2–3 years** |
| Legal | Land tenure/title, holding registration, licences, debts/liens, existing insurance, employment status of current staff, legal entity |
| People | Is there a manager today? Staff count, wages, literacy/languages, housing, relatives among staff or suppliers |
| Infrastructure | Water source and yield, power, mobile signal at each site, fences, handling facilities, housing, storage |
| Markets | Buyers, contracts, channels (cash/farm-gate?), certification demands |
| Owner | Budget and risk tolerance; what "without daily involvement" means (weekly call? visits?); realistic available hours; trusted local contacts; named deputy/representative candidates; will, power of attorney, bank signatories |
| Authority | Competent veterinary authority, 24-h number, **country notifiable-disease list** (from the authority, not from this plan) |

Exit: all rows answered or marked "unknown, baseline audit to resolve"; baseline audit report received.

## 3. Gates and exit criteria

| Gate | When | Exit criteria (all required) |
|---|---|---|
| G0 Intake | wk 0–3 | Section 2 complete; baseline audit done; lawyer engaged on entity/tenure; manager longlist and local wage benchmark started (no offer yet) |
| G1 Viability and scope | wk 3–6 | (a) one-page budget per enterprise at actual scale in 5 scenarios (base; price −20% and yield −20%; drought −30% yield; disease −10% stock; inputs +25%); (b) **net margin after full management and control cost >= 0 in base** and cash never below the floor with reserve in stress cases; (c) controls and oversight cost <= ~8–10% of gross revenue (default); (d) feed, water and labour models pass (Section 4); (e) options appraisal done; (f) owner signs the enterprise list and states the annual "cost of absence" he accepts |
| G2 Design and verification | wk 6–16 | SOPs for retained enterprises signed per Section 9; each SOP completed from the sheet alone by a worker who did not write it; entity, bank mandates, insurance, vendor/employee master files in place; 5 tabletop scenarios passed (outbreak, theft, manager quits, owner unreachable, power/water failure); alert chain tested end to end |
| G3 Pilot | >= 8 weeks, in season | Data completeness >= 95%; stock within ±2% of independent count; cash above floor; zero unreported incidents found by audit; two unannounced audits clean; owner hours within budget (rolling 8 weeks) |
| G4 Staged autonomy | >= 12 months | See below |

**Staged autonomy.** L1: owner approves all Tier 2+ items and reviews weekly. L2: monthly review, exception alerts. L3: quarterly review plus permanent minimum oversight. Advance L1→L2 after G3; L2→L3 only after one **full annual cycle** containing a breeding/birthing event, weaning, a planting and harvest, annual stock count and tax close, and at least one real manager leave period with the deputy in charge. If a cycle element has not occurred, the period extends. A consultant vet/stockman is retained as backstop for the whole first cycle. **Permanent minimum** at L3: bank mandate, independent bank/supplier feeds, annual external audit, 2–4 unannounced visits a year, quarterly alert-chain test.

**Kill/pivot triggers (defaults):** cumulative loss beyond the amount signed at G1; two failed audits; second manager exit in 12 months; owner hours exceeding budget by 50% for a quarter. Any trigger forces an owner decision: scale down, lease out, or exit.

## 4. Viability, rationalization and the shared model
**Shared Farm Model (WP0, built first, consumed by all WPs):** herd/flock dynamics, monthly feed balance (energy, protein, forage, bought-in tonnes), labour hours by month, water demand, 24-month cash flow.
- **Feed:** budget per enterprise per month vs farm production; decide which crops are feed and which are cash. Bought-in protein is assumed until shown otherwise. Stored grain <= 13% moisture; aflatoxin testing rule; 2 months' feed reserve; **destock trigger when feed-days remaining < 60** (default).
- **Water:** demand in the driest month of a 1-in-10 year vs reliable supply (defaults: cattle 30–50 L/d, small ruminants 5–10, poultry 0.25–0.4 L/bird plus cooling); backup storage >= 24–48 h for all stock; legal abstraction checked.
- **Labour:** hours by task and month; 7-day coverage needs ~1.5–2 FTE per post; birthing-season surge and night watch; split birthing seasons the team can staff; casual labour at pre-agreed rates. If the plan cannot be staffed, enterprises are cut. This is a constraint on enterprise choice, not an afterthought.
- **Enterprise rationalization (G1):** rank each enterprise by gross margin per labour-hour, supervision need, capital, absence tolerance and downside. **Default presumption: at most 2–3 enterprises in year 1**; others are leased out, contracted out, sold down or deferred until 12 months of clean records. Poultry needs positive justification (highest mortality and fraud sensitivity; layers vs broilers decided here, with market, slaughter/cold-chain and licensing named). Where the farm already runs all five, "sell down" is a managed transition timed to the season, not an abrupt stop.
- **Options appraisal** (NPV and owner-hours): self-run with manager, manager-operator with equity or profit share, lease land, share-farming/contract grazing, management company, sell.

## 5. Authority, controls and anti-fraud architecture
**Legal and roles.** Lawyer advises on a separate farm entity (owner director; manager an employee, never director or signatory), employment contracts, labour-law compliance, licences held by the right party, and owner liability. Manager is a **hire with vetting**: references including non-nominated ones, lawful background checks, probation with heightened checks, relationships declared. Contract: 90-day notice, return of records and credentials, confidentiality, clawback of deferred bonus on misconduct (enforceability per lawyer). Deputy is named, trained and unrelated to the manager; scale decides whether the deputy is a salaried post or a part-time stand-in.

**Authority tiers (defaults; AOB = annual operating budget).**

| Tier | Who | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| T0 | Unit lead | <= 0.05% AOB per item, 0.2% per 30 days |
| T1 | Manager | <= 0.3% AOB per item; <= 1.5% per 30 days per supplier and per category; within budget line |
| T2 | Manager proposes, owner/representative approves | Above T1, any budget-line overrun, new supplier above threshold, contracts < 12 months |
| T3 | Owner only | Capital items, asset sales/write-offs, borrowing, contracts >= 12 months, hire/fire/pay of manager and deputy, related-party deals, insurance settlements, vendor bank-detail changes (call-back to known number) |
| Emergency | Manager, then vet/deputy if owner and representative silent 2 h | <= 1% AOB per event for disease, water, power, welfare; owner informed within 24 h |

Splitting is prohibited and tested at audit. The charter has a **"manager cannot" list**: release payments, hold bank signing rights, be sole recorder of stock, be sole source of dashboard data, be sole contact with buyers on price.

**Segregation of duties.** Requester (unit lead) / approver (manager) / receiver (different person, goods-received note with count) / payer (owner or representative releases; external bookkeeper prepares, never releases). Three-way match before payment. To protect owner time, a weekly payment batch and a **whitelist** of standing payments (payroll, utilities, approved recurring suppliers within budget) release on a single owner/representative approval; everything else needs it per item.

**Cash and bank.** Owner and representative hold mandates; manager has a capped card/wallet topped up against reconciled spend. Buyers pay direct to farm account; unavoidable cash uses serialised receipts, two-person count, banking within 48 h. Bank statements go straight to owner and external accountant. Petty cash is imprest with a hard cap and weekly count by a non-custodian.

**Stock and inventory.** Monthly signed reconciliation (opening + births + purchases − deaths − sales − slaughter = closing). Each death: timestamped photo of tagged carcass, tag retained, witness above a value threshold, disposal logged. Gate pass and two-person load-out for every movement. Drug/vaccine register with batch numbers, two-key store, vet reconciliation to purchases. Feed and grain by calibrated count at receipt and issue, bin dips. Variance triggers at 3–5% unexplained, plus feed-conversion and hen-day benchmarks; the low steady drip is caught by 4-week rolling trends, not spike alerts.

**Audit (resolves cost vs independence).** Owner appoints, pays and instructs the auditor; reports go to the owner first. Not peers. Tiered by scale and costed at G1: external bookkeeper does monthly bank/supplier reconciliation from direct feeds; **unannounced, randomised spot-counts 6–12 times a year** (rotating non-peer, e.g. agri-accountant junior or vet-tech) counting live animals against tags, payroll muster, procurement against market price, sales against bank; annual full review by a licensed accountant with bank/supplier/buyer confirmations. Auditors rotate at most every 2 years and declare conflicts. Vet who prescribes does not also certify deaths for insurance; vet invoices go direct to bookkeeper and owner.

**Payroll.** Employee master file held by external bookkeeper with ID and photo; changes need owner approval; pay only to the employee's own account/wallet; attendance by geotagged check-in or auditor muster rather than supervisor say-so; casual day sheets countersigned and checked against output; annual headcount-to-payroll match.

**Procurement and sales.** Three quotes above threshold, annual re-tender of feed/drugs/seed/fuel, yearly conflict declarations, owner-approved preferred list. Minimum price list, owner-signed larger contracts, credit sales approved by owner, buyer weigh-in reconciled to weigh-out. Asset register with serials, vehicle logs, fuel dip reconciliation, application records for inputs reconciled to purchases.

**Incentives.** Bonuses paid only on **audited** figures, 50% deferred to the next audit with clawback, multi-year measures (herd value, pasture/soil condition, asset condition), a welfare/safety gate, and an audit-integrity multiplier. Paired KPIs (mortality with audited counts and cause evidence; weaning/lambing/calving %, ADG, FCR, conversion, treatment rate). No bonus on self-reported production.

**Data integrity.** Owner's account owns all data stores, cameras and domains; manager is editor with no delete; append-only log with user, timestamp, photo/GPS on deaths, deliveries, sales, feed issues. Independent feeds the manager cannot touch: bank, supplier statements, vet invoices, fuel and power meters, gate records. Quarterly cross-check of dashboard to feeds. Camera and biometric use only after privacy-law check, with notices.

**Oversight resilience.** Named **owner's representative** (trusted person or paid farm steward, scaled to farm size) with review rights and a second bank mandate. Escalation ladder with deadlines: owner 30 min (daytime) → representative 2 h → vet/adviser within emergency envelope. Power of attorney and a standing instruction letter for owner incapacity; one-page "if I am gone" file with the representative; will/trust succession documented. Code of conduct signed by all staff, anonymous whistle-blower channel (third-party hotline if feasible) with no-retaliation rule, investigations by someone outside the line. **First-72-hours playbook** for sudden manager exit: bank control, stock count, store security, staff briefing, credentials revoked within 1 hour, keys re-set. Annual review of the control set; any override logged to the owner; fraud risks in the risk register with named owners. Retention: financial 7 years or local law; animal and drug records per regulator; off-site backup owned by owner.

**Insurance (lawyer/broker).** Fidelity/crime (manager and bookkeeper), employer's and public liability, property, equipment, motor, livestock/crop where available, and confirmation that notifiable-disease compensation depends on timely reporting. Insurers' tag/records requirements are built into the ID system. Claims run only by owner or representative with vet/authority corroboration.

## 6. Animal health, biosecurity and notifiable disease
**Notifiable-disease design.** Gate 0 names the authority, 24-h contact and national list. The **suspect list** (default triggers, vet to confirm): sudden deaths with no cause; blood from orifices or an unopened carcass; vesicles, drooling, severe lameness; abortion cluster (>= 2 in 7 days); neurological cases; poultry mortality jump or egg-drop/respiratory signs. Response, in order: (1) **standstill** on animals, carcasses, milk, eggs, manure, vehicles and people leaving; (2) **do not open the carcass**; (3) manager, deputy or any shift lead calls the vet and the authority; (4) owner and vet are notified automatically within the hour. The reporting duty rests with the licensed holder, but reporting never waits for an owner reply. Sample-submission protocol, culling/compensation steps and an HPAI housing order for free-range birds are in the vet-signed playbook. Owner crisis time is budgeted (Section 8).

**Biosecurity layout (decided at G1).** Poultry zone away from ruminants (default >= 50–100 m, vet to set), separate staff, boots and tools, one-way flow, hard door, foot dips, wild-bird and rodent exclusion, covered feed, no open surface water, visitor/vehicle log signed off weekly. All-in/all-out with >= 14 days downtime. If separation or dedicated staff is impossible, poultry is dropped or contracted out. Incoming stock: certified source, health certificate, test per vet, **quarantine default 30 days** apart from the herd, quarantine drench. Ruminant staff flow: youngest/cleanest first.

**Species rules (retained species only).** Separate chapters. Sheep never get cattle/goat mineral (copper); goats treated as browsers with distinct dosing; colostrum rule (default 10% of body weight within 6 h); lambing/kidding/calving supervision and night watch; clostridial vaccination; pregnancy toxaemia and urinary calculi controls; bloat and milk fever plans; predator plan. **Worms:** selective treatment guided by faecal egg counts, resistance test at set-up and annually, trained scorers with competency check, no blanket drenching. Tick, footrot and fly-strike calendars from the vet's regional disease list. Breeding: defined mating window, pregnancy scanning at 40–60 days, body-condition targets, culling rules, abortion investigation (ties to suspect list).

**Poultry specifics.** Flock vaccination calendar keyed to placement date, certified hatchery, litter/composting rule (about 55 °C for >= 3 days; 90–120 days before ground-contact edible crops). Ventilation and power failure: generator with automatic changeover, weekly test, manual ventilation fallback; closed houses can fail within 15–30 minutes in heat.

**Medicines.** Vet-of-record with written relationship and treatment protocols; treatment only on vet or manager order; locked register reconciled monthly; every treatment sets a withdrawal date, a physical mark and a **no-sale flag** in the records; vaccine fridge on backup power with alarmed logger; sharps rule. Off-label (e.g. goats) withdrawal set by the vet.

**Worker safety, welfare, disposal, ID.** Zoonosis SOP (PPE for abortions, no raw milk, pregnant-worker policy), induction training, nobody alone with bulls or in a crush, first aid and transport plan, workers' compensation cover. Trained person authorised to euthanise on every shift. Fallen-stock method per local law (lined pit/compost away from water, vet post-mortem above a death threshold, pre-identified mass-mortality site). Individual ID, registered holding, birth/death/sale/movement register, **no animal moves without a signed movement document**. Facility checklist per species, daily visual water check, third-party liability.

**Crops (agronomist).** Baseline soil test by management unit (two depths, repeated every 2–3 years), manure and nutrient budget with storage for the closed season, scouting with phone-photo reports and action thresholds, approved pesticide list with intervals/PPE, no spraying near poultry or water, hermetic storage and a sell-or-store rule, forage budget with hay/silage made in the labour-peak season, labour histogram checked against rosters.

## 7. Alerts and thresholds (defaults; vet/agronomist calibrate; baselines replace them after 8–12 weeks)

| Signal | Amber | Red (call now) |
|---|---|---|
| Broiler mortality (after week 1) | > 0.5%/day | > 1% in 2 days, or doubling in 24 h |
| Layer mortality / production | > 0.1%/day | Egg drop >= 10% in 2 days or feed/water intake drop > 10%; doubling in 24 h |
| Adult ruminants | >= 2 unexplained deaths/7 days | Any sudden unexplained death; >= 2 abortions/7 days (notifiable-suspect rules apply) |
| Young stock | > 1.5× baseline | Cluster of deaths in one group |
| Shrinkage/variance | 3% unexplained | 5%, or failed spot-count |
| Water/power/cold chain | Reserve < 48 h | Reserve < 24 h; poultry house over-temperature or power loss; vaccine fridge outside 2–8 °C for > 30 min |
| Cash | Within 120% of floor | Below floor |
| **Silence** | Daily mortality form missing by set time | No data for 24 h → deputy; 48 h → representative visits |

Dashboard is capped at 8–12 KPIs, each with measurement method and a data owner. Alerts are tiered (now / next day / weekly). False-positive and miss rates reviewed quarterly.

## 8. Technology and owner time
**Tech last, minimal.** Start with paper tally sheets, weekly photo/SMS upload and a manager-compiled report into an owner-owned store; add digital forms only where a connectivity survey passes. Pictorial/local-language field pack tested with workers, separate from the owner pack (English, short). **Exception (justified by loss avoided):** life-critical alarms (poultry house temperature and power, vaccine fridge, water reserve) run on **stand-alone GSM/SMS siren-and-call units**, not on data. Required fallbacks: offline-capable forms; SMS/voice path; two recipients per alert; **dead-man's switch** (no check-in → escalation); power-out-for-3-days and expired-SIM drills; paper procedures for power and water failure. Each sensor gets a cost-benefit and calibration budget. Stata/R templates dropped.

**Owner time budget (defaults; logged in the pilot).** Routine 1.5–2 h/week (dashboard, payment batch, alerts); monthly 3 h; quarterly 1 day (audit, budget, alert-chain test); crisis reserve 2–3 days a quarter; site presence in year 1 about 3–4 weeks total including gates; afterward 2–4 unannounced visits a year. Rolling 8-week owner hours are a gate metric. Representative covers when the owner is unavailable.

## 10. Season-indexed critical path (today 2026-10-15)
Season logic depends on Gate 0 location; **choose one column after G0.** Anything not irreversible continues as the incumbent manager planned.

| Window | N. temperate | S. temperate | Tropics/monsoon (rain regime decides) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Now–Nov 2026 | Autumn breeding/tupping and winter feed budget; check winter housing, order vaccines; winter cereals sown | Spring: lambing/calving under way (labour surge, predators, vet backstop); summer crops planting now (seed, soil test, inputs must already be on site); fly-strike season | Rabi/short-rains planting or harvest and dry-season start; destock check on feed-days |
| Dec–Feb | Winter feeding, order spring seed 6–8 wks ahead, start G2 with housed stock | Hay/silage, weaning, shearing, heat-stress cover for poultry | Dry-season feed and water; G2 work |
| Mar–May | Calving/lambing/kidding (surge staffing), spring planting | Autumn breeding, harvest, tax close if applicable | Main rains planting, tick/vector peak |
| Jun–Sep | Harvest, hay/silage, weaning, annual count | Winter feeding, order seed | Harvest, drying/storage |

Rule: **do not launch new enterprises, restock poultry or sign long contracts inside a peak** (birthing, planting, harvest). Lead times (default): seed and fertiliser 4–8 weeks, chicks 6–8 weeks, manager recruitment 3–6 months, insurance and entity set-up 2–3 months. Manager recruitment search starts week 1; the offer waits for G1.
