# Appendix A: Health, biosecurity, alerts, feed and crops (design defaults)

**Not for approval now.** These are design defaults to be calibrated and signed at G1/G2 (see PLAN.md sections 3–4). Every figure is a default; the vet or agronomist replaces it. Retained enterprises only.

## A0. Day-7 safety pack (G0 exit criterion; baseline vet owns it)
1. Vet-of-record with 24-h cover (poultry-competent or a lab route if poultry exist); authority number verified by live call; fallback to the authority vet if the vet is unreachable in 2 h.
2. One-page suspect / standstill / do-not-open card at each site. **Suspicion reporting by any person never waits** for the lawyer's confirmation that the manager may report for the holder.
3. Owner letter pre-authorising emergency spend and reporting.
4. Named euthanasia person. Means and licence (firearm or captive bolt; poultry method) are a **G0 exit** criterion, not Day 7; until then the on-call route is the vet or a licensed neighbour/contractor, and the named person is a fallback.
5. Water, power and vaccine-fridge status check; drug-store inventory reconciled.
6. **Interim withdrawal check** on every sale or dispatch until the gate-pass line exists.
7. **Live-season check** (one page): birthing arrangements, night watch, colostrum, clostridial cover, predators, ionophore and copper separation; a named stockperson for the peak.
8. **If poultry exist:** daily mortality count to the coordinator, the A2 red rule as interim rule, generator test and curtain/ventilation fallback. **Interim biosecurity minimum (baseline vet signs):** covered feed, no contact with surface water or wild birds, boots and visitor log, poultry visited last, check of local movement or housing orders. No litter on sheep pasture or in feed; medicated feed kept off shared equipment (the interim lead operates these until the manager starts).
9. **Interim treatment list:** the baseline vet issues a one-page list (what the incumbent may give without a call, what needs a call, critical antimicrobials vet-only), entered in the drug register with the same no-sale flag.

## A1. Notifiable disease and sudden death
Gate 0 names the authority, 24-h contact and national notifiable list. The reporting duty rests with the licensed holder and **never waits for an owner reply**. Act first, notify in parallel: the manager acts and calls the vet immediately within the emergency envelope; the ladder (owner, representative, adviser) governs who is informed and who may extend the envelope, not whether the first response happens.

**Immediate standstill and report (no triage)** on: vesicles with lameness or drooling in several animals; blood from orifices or unclotted blood from a carcass; abortion storms (rule in A2, never below the national rule); poultry with swollen head/comb, or respiratory signs in multiple birds of a house with deaths at or above amber, or egg drop >=10% together with deaths at or above the red floor (6 birds per house per day or the percentage, whichever is larger). **Neurological signs, skin nodules/pox lesions, drooling and mouth erosions:** report when several animals are affected, signs are generalised, or the vet cannot exclude a notifiable cause; a single animal is isolated and gets vet triage within 2 h. Standstill covers animals, carcasses, milk, eggs, manure, vehicles and people; non-essential visits (auditors, buyers) are suspended; a vet-approved exemption list covers essential feed and water deliveries, milk and egg collection, animal-care labour and carcass removal.

**Two-step triage for an isolated sudden death (ruminants):**
1. Leave the carcass unopened and covered, **excluding scavengers, dogs and livestock**; photograph in situ with the ear tag legible; **leave the tag on** (tag removal and sampling by the vet or authority only). Vet phone triage within 2 h, else the authority vet. No standstill at this step.
2. The vet or authority escalates to report and standstill when: anthrax-endemic region or history; in adults, >=2 deaths in one group (same paddock, shed or age cohort) within 24–48 h; in young stock, only survivor signs (fever, vesicles, neurological signs, bleeding) or no attributable cause; bleeding from orifices; fever, vesicles or neurological signs in survivors; or the vet cannot attribute a cause.

**Poultry triage mirrors this:** a red poultry threshold means house lockdown (no movement in or out of the house) and vet phone triage within 2 h; the report and standstill follow only on the poultry signs above or when the vet cannot attribute a cause. A power or temperature failure is handled as an operational welfare emergency (generator, ventilation fallback, cooling) and is not a notifiable report unless disease signs are present.

Cause stays "unexplained" until a vet or authority post-mortem. The mortality KPI and any bonus use vet-confirmed cause only. G2 tabletop includes a live call to the authority and vet numbers, sample-submission and out-of-hours contacts. Insurance: confirm whether compensation depends on timely reporting.

## A2. Alert thresholds (vet/agronomist-calibrated defaults)
Baselines may **tighten** red triggers. **Only the vet may reset a red default, in writing, after a documented baseline** (for example free-range or predation-heavy layers). Young-stock baselines must be seasonal or benchmark-based.

| Signal | Amber | Red (call now) |
|---|---|---|
| Broilers, after week 1 | >0.2%/day, or 2x the flock's trailing 7-day mean | >0.5%/day, or doubling in 24 h **with at least the floor below** |
| Week 1 (broilers, day-old pullets) | >1.0% cumulative by day 7: vet review | Placeholder: >1%/day or >2% in 2 days (vet-replaceable) |
| Layers | >0.05%/day; egg drop >=10% in 2 days; intake drop >=10% | >0.1%/day, or doubling in 24 h **with the floor**, or intake drop >=20% over 2 days, or egg drop >=20%; or amber egg-drop/intake signs together with deaths at or above the red floor (6 birds or the percentage, whichever is larger) |
| Floor (all flock sizes) | Amber and red apply at the **larger of** N birds per house per day (default 3 / 6) and the percentage; doubling counts only above the larger of 10 birds and 0.1% of the flock. Small flocks (and all week-1 broods under ~300 birds, which use the percentage leg instead of the N-bird floor) also have a 7-day cumulative leg (**where both the week-1 row and this leg apply, the stricter applies**), vet-replaceable: amber >5% (vet call within 24 h), red >10% | |
| **Poultry red means** | | Lockdown and vet phone triage within 2 h (A1); reporting only on the specific signs |
| Adult ruminants | >=2 unexplained deaths in 7 days | Two-step triage (A1) |
| Abortions | Expressed as a count or percentage of females at risk, set by the vet | Never below the national reporting rule; where a single abortion is reportable, one is red |
| Young stock | >1.5x seasonal baseline | Vet call within 2 h when >=2 deaths in one group (same paddock, shed or age cohort) within 24–48 h, excluding first-days neonatal deaths inside the seasonal baseline. Standstill only on the A1 step-2 young-stock signs (survivor signs or no attributable cause) |
| Shrinkage/variance | 3% unexplained | 5%, or failed spot-count (starts B8) |
| Water reserve | Below 75% of designed storage | Below 50% of designed storage |
| Power/cold chain | Generator test failed | House over-temperature or power loss (welfare emergency, not a notifiable report); vaccine fridge outside 2-8 C for >30 min |
| Feed-days (projected to season end) | <120 | <90 (reserve plus lead time; owner pre-authorises the staged response) |
| Rainfall or pasture growth vs season-point | Shortfall: stop buying breeding stock, early weaning | Larger shortfall: early cull of bottom class, sell store animals, then full destock |
| Stored grain | Moisture >13% | Hot spot or insects |
| Scouting | Threshold reached | Threshold breached after treatment |
| Cash | Within 120% of floor | Below floor |
| **Silence** | Daily mortality form missing by set time | No data 24 h to the deputy; 48 h the representative visits |

Feed-days and rainfall have a named data owner (agronomist-supplied season-points; manager measures; spot-counts verify). The 8–12 KPI cap is kept by retiring others.

## A3. Biosecurity, species rules, medicines
- **Layout (decided at G1):** poultry zone away from ruminants (default >=50–100 m, vet sets), separate staff, boots and tools, one-way flow, hard door, foot dips, wild-bird and rodent exclusion, covered feed, no open surface water, visitor/vehicle log reviewed weekly. All-in/all-out, >=14 days downtime. If separation or dedicated staff is impossible, drop or contract out poultry.
- **Incomers:** certified source, health certificate, tests per vet, quarantine default 30 days apart, quarantine drench. **Auditors and visitors** follow clean-to-dirty, poultry last or separate visits, boots and clothing.
- **Species chapters:** no cattle/goat mineral to sheep (copper); goats treated as browsers with distinct dosing; colostrum rule (species-specific, vet sets; default 10% of bodyweight within 6 h); lambing/kidding/calving supervision and night watch; clostridial vaccination; pregnancy toxaemia, urinary calculi, bloat, milk fever plans; predator plan; tick, footrot, fly-strike calendars from the vet's regional list.
- **Worms:** selective treatment guided by faecal egg counts (name who runs them and where), annual resistance test, trained scorers with competency check. **Vet-defined exceptions** to selective treatment: high-risk classes and periods, an outbreak, lab delay beyond a stated number of days.
- **Poultry health:** vet-signed vaccination programme (Newcastle, Marek, IBD, fowl pox, coccidiosis by region) keyed to placement date, routine necropsy route, and a housing-order response for free-range birds in the vet's playbook.
- **Breeding:** defined mating window, scanning timing species-specific (vet sets), body-condition targets, culling rules.
- **Medicines:** vet-of-record with written relationship and treatment protocols; treatment by **manager order within the vet's written protocol only**; critical antimicrobials vet-only, treatment rate on the KPI list; locked two-key register reconciled monthly; each treatment sets a withdrawal date and a no-sale flag, with a physical mark for animals and a **flock/house-level flag for eggs**. **The signed gate pass and egg/milk dispatch sheet carry a mandatory withdrawal-check line** (animal ID against register, mark seen) completed by the second person. Vaccine fridge on backup power with alarmed logger; sharps rule. Off-label withdrawal set by the vet. If there is only one vet, a second practice or the authority vet certifies deaths.
- **Safety and welfare:** zoonosis SOP (PPE for abortions, no raw milk, pregnant-worker policy), induction, rabies exposure (bites) protocol where relevant, poultry-outbreak PPE and worker symptom reporting to public health, no one touches a suspect carcass, nobody alone with bulls or in a crush, first aid and transport plan, workers' compensation. A trained, legally licensed person authorised to euthanise on every shift; fit-to-load rule; castration, dehorning and docking only within legal limits with pain relief; milk and egg hygiene SOP; egg registration, grading and salmonella programme where the country requires them.
- **Fallen stock:** method per local law (lined pit or compost away from water, vet post-mortem above a death threshold, pre-identified mass-mortality site, disinfectant and PPE stockpile). Individual ID, registered holding, movement register; no movement without a signed document.

## A4. Cross-enterprise feed, litter and chemical rule (operated by the interim lead, then the manager; vet and agronomist co-sign)
- Dedicated or flushed-and-logged equipment for medicated poultry feed; separate labelled stores and scoops per species; poultry feed (ionophore coccidiostats) never reaches cattle, sheep or goats; no litter in ruminant feed.
- Litter/manure spreading map with a grazing exclusion interval (vet/agronomist set) and no litter on sheep pasture unless tested (copper); litter composted by a method the vet or agronomist sets (windrow systems need longer and turning than in-vessel's about 55 C for >=3 days) and kept off ground-contact edible crops for 90–120 days; storage sized for the closed season; nutrient budget by soil test.
- Medicated and top-dressed feeds enter the same register as medicines, with the same no-sale flag. Approved-chemicals list has a livestock grazing/feeding-interval column linked to that flag; no spraying near poultry or water; treated-seed handling; poisonous-plant paddock check; water-quality test at baseline.

## A5. Crops, forage and drought
Baseline soil test per unit (two depths; repeat every 2–3 years); scouting with phone photos and action thresholds; hermetic storage and a sell-or-store rule; stocking set from measured forage, with hay/silage made in the labour-peak season; labour histogram checked against rosters; machinery and contractor capacity by operation per week, pre-booked contractors, spares. Water model adds irrigation demand (mm/ha) where irrigated and abstraction permits. Distressed sales are avoided by the leading triggers in A2, with 4–8 weeks of movement lead time built in. **Every staged response row is pre-authorised in the G1-signed drought plan with sale ceilings**, so it does not wait for the owner; interim season-points come from the window memo. Yield verification: calibrated-trailer or weighbridge tickets at harvest, GPS field areas, benchmark cross-check in spot-counts, buyer weigh-in reconciled to store out-turn.

## A6. Spot-counts and standstill
Late-pregnancy animals are counted in situ without mustering. Auditors follow the biosecurity route. Count tolerance: ±2% is the default only for fully mustered or housed counts; otherwise it is the method's error plus reconciled births, deaths and movements. During a standstill spot-counts are replaced by remote verification (death photos, bank, feed and drug records, vet records) and a catch-up count on release. Feed-flow and litter controls (A4) are operated by the interim lead until the manager starts, then the manager, with unit leads executing.

## A7. Farm Model input tags
Every input is tagged measured / regional benchmark / assumed, with a version log and a named model owner. Re-confirmation triggers: first harvest, first dry season, first birthing season.
