# Appendix A: Health, biosecurity, alerts, feed and crops (design defaults)

**Not for approval now.** These are design defaults to be calibrated and signed at G1/G2 (see PLAN.md section 8). Every figure is a default; the vet or agronomist replaces it. Retained enterprises only.

## A1. Notifiable disease and sudden death
Gate 0 names the authority, 24-h contact and national notifiable list. The reporting duty rests with the licensed holder and **never waits for an owner reply**. Act first, notify in parallel: the manager acts and calls the vet immediately within the emergency envelope; the ladder (owner, representative, adviser) governs who is informed and who may extend the envelope, not whether the first response happens.

**Immediate standstill and report (no triage)** on any of: vesicles, drooling, severe herd lameness; skin nodules or pox lesions; mouth erosions with oculonasal discharge and diarrhoea; blood from orifices or unclotted blood from a carcass; neurological cases; abortion above the rule in A2 (never below the national rule); poultry with swollen head/comb, respiratory signs, egg drop, or a red poultry threshold. Standstill covers animals, carcasses, milk, eggs, manure, vehicles and people; a vet-approved exemption lists essential feed and water deliveries.

**Two-step triage for an isolated sudden death (ruminants):**
1. Leave the carcass unopened and covered; photograph in situ with the ear tag legible; **leave the tag on** (tag removal and sampling by the vet or authority only). Vet phone triage within 2 h. No standstill at this step.
2. The vet or authority escalates to report and standstill when: anthrax-endemic region or history; >=2 deaths in a group within 24–48 h; bleeding from orifices; fever, vesicles or neurological signs in survivors; or the vet cannot attribute a cause.

Cause stays "unexplained" until a vet or authority post-mortem. The mortality KPI and any bonus use vet-confirmed cause only. G2 tabletop includes a live call to the authority and vet numbers, sample-submission and out-of-hours contacts. Insurance: confirm whether compensation depends on timely reporting.

## A2. Alert thresholds (vet/agronomist-calibrated defaults)
Baselines may **tighten** red triggers but never loosen them. Young-stock baselines must be seasonal or benchmark-based.

| Signal | Amber | Red (call now) |
|---|---|---|
| Broilers, after week 1 | >0.2%/day, or 2x the flock's trailing 7-day mean | >0.5%/day, or doubling in 24 h, or an absolute count set by the vet |
| Broilers, week 1 | >1.0% cumulative by day 7: vet review | |
| Layers | >0.05%/day | >0.1%/day, or doubling in 24 h, or egg drop >=10% in 2 days, or feed/water intake drop >10% |
| Small flocks (<~2,000 birds) | >=3 birds/day in a house | >=6 birds/day: "N birds or the percentage, whichever is smaller" |
| **Poultry red means** | | House lockdown, vet call, and the notifiable-suspect response in A1 |
| Adult ruminants | >=2 unexplained deaths in 7 days | Two-step triage (A1) |
| Abortions | Expressed as a count or percentage of females at risk, set by the vet | Never below the national reporting rule; where a single abortion is reportable, one is red |
| Young stock | >1.5x seasonal baseline | Cluster of deaths in one group |
| Shrinkage/variance | 3% unexplained | 5%, or failed spot-count (starts B8) |
| Water reserve | Below 75% of designed storage | Below 50% of designed storage |
| Power/cold chain | Generator test failed | House over-temperature or power loss; vaccine fridge outside 2-8 C for >30 min |
| Feed-days (projected to season end) | <120 | <90 (reserve plus lead time; owner pre-authorises the staged response) |
| Rainfall or pasture growth vs season-point | Shortfall: stop buying breeding stock, early weaning | Larger shortfall: early cull of bottom class, sell store animals, then full destock |
| Stored grain | Moisture >13% | Hot spot or insects |
| Scouting | Threshold reached | Threshold breached after treatment |
| Cash | Within 120% of floor | Below floor |
| **Silence** | Daily mortality form missing by set time | No data 24 h to the deputy; 48 h the representative visits |

Feed-days and rainfall have a named data owner (agronomist-supplied season-points; manager measures; spot-counts verify). The 8–12 KPI cap is kept by retiring others.

## A3. Biosecurity, species rules, medicines
- **Layout (decided at G1):** poultry zone away from ruminants (default >=50–100 m, vet sets), separate staff, boots and tools, one-way flow, hard door, foot dips, wild-bird and rodent exclusion, covered feed, no open surface water, visitor/vehicle log reviewed weekly. All-in/all-out, >=14 days downtime. If separation or dedicated staff is impossible, drop or contract out poultry.
- **Incomers:** certified source, health certificate, tests per vet, quarantine default 30 days apart, quarantine drench. **Auditors and visitors** follow clean-to-dirty, poultry last or separate visits, boots and clothing.
- **Species chapters:** no cattle/goat mineral to sheep (copper); goats treated as browsers with distinct dosing; colostrum rule (default 10% of bodyweight within 6 h); lambing/kidding/calving supervision and night watch; clostridial vaccination; pregnancy toxaemia, urinary calculi, bloat, milk fever plans; predator plan; tick, footrot, fly-strike calendars from the vet's regional list.
- **Worms:** selective treatment guided by faecal egg counts (name who runs them and where), annual resistance test, trained scorers with competency check. **Vet-defined exceptions** to selective treatment: high-risk classes and periods, an outbreak, lab delay beyond a stated number of days.
- **Breeding:** defined mating window, scanning at 40–60 days, body-condition targets, culling rules.
- **Medicines:** vet-of-record with written relationship and treatment protocols; treatment only on vet or manager order; locked two-key register reconciled monthly; each treatment sets a withdrawal date, a physical mark and a no-sale flag. **The signed gate pass and egg/milk dispatch sheet carry a mandatory withdrawal-check line** (animal ID against register, mark seen) completed by the second person. Vaccine fridge on backup power with alarmed logger; sharps rule. Off-label withdrawal set by the vet. If there is only one vet, a second practice or the authority vet certifies deaths.
- **Safety and welfare:** zoonosis SOP (PPE for abortions, no raw milk, pregnant-worker policy), induction, nobody alone with bulls or in a crush, first aid and transport plan, workers' compensation. A trained, legally licensed person authorised to euthanise on every shift; fit-to-load rule; castration, dehorning and docking only within legal limits with pain relief; milk and egg hygiene SOP; egg registration, grading and salmonella programme where the country requires them.
- **Fallen stock:** method per local law (lined pit or compost away from water, vet post-mortem above a death threshold, pre-identified mass-mortality site, disinfectant and PPE stockpile). Individual ID, registered holding, movement register; no movement without a signed document.

## A4. Cross-enterprise feed, litter and chemical rule (owned by WP4, vet and agronomist co-sign)
- Dedicated or flushed-and-logged equipment for medicated poultry feed; separate labelled stores and scoops per species; poultry feed (ionophore coccidiostats) never reaches cattle, sheep or goats; no litter in ruminant feed.
- Litter/manure spreading map with a grazing exclusion interval (vet/agronomist set) and no litter on sheep pasture unless tested (copper); litter composted (about 55 C for >=3 days) and kept off ground-contact edible crops for 90–120 days; storage sized for the closed season; nutrient budget by soil test.
- Medicated and top-dressed feeds enter the same register as medicines, with the same no-sale flag. Approved-chemicals list has a livestock grazing/feeding-interval column linked to that flag; no spraying near poultry or water; treated-seed handling; poisonous-plant paddock check; water-quality test at baseline.

## A5. Crops, forage and drought
Baseline soil test per unit (two depths; repeat every 2–3 years); scouting with phone photos and action thresholds; hermetic storage and a sell-or-store rule; stocking set from measured forage, with hay/silage made in the labour-peak season; labour histogram checked against rosters; machinery and contractor capacity by operation per week, pre-booked contractors, spares. Water model adds irrigation demand (mm/ha) where irrigated and abstraction permits. Distressed sales are avoided by the leading triggers in A2, with 4–8 weeks of movement lead time built into the staged response. Yield verification: calibrated-trailer or weighbridge tickets at harvest, GPS field areas, benchmark cross-check in spot-counts, buyer weigh-in reconciled to store out-turn.

## A6. Farm Model input tags
Every input is tagged measured / regional benchmark / assumed, with a version log and a named model owner. Re-confirmation triggers: first harvest, first dry season, first birthing season.
