# Review R2: Agronomy and Cross-Enterprise Integration (adversarial, round 2)

Scope: PLAN.md v2 against r1-poultry-crops.md. Resolved and not re-raised: poultry decision and location gate (G0/G1), enterprise rationalization, biosecurity layout, notifiable disease, mortality thresholds (broiler/layer split), power/water SPOF and GSM alarms, cold chain, drug withdrawal flag, early feed budget, labour model and histogram, water budget (stock), seasons table and lead times, verification layer, soil testing cadence, scouting, post-harvest, composting rule. Items that wait on Gate 0 owner intake are not counted as major.

Assessment of prior MAJORs: P1-P10 and P13 are genuinely fixed at plan level. P11 (seasons/today) and P12 (climate/water) are only partly fixed; the gaps are items 1, 3 and 5 below. New contradictions come mostly from the gate timeline colliding with the season table (item 2) and from integration paths the plan names but does not control (item 4).

## MAJOR

1. [MAJOR] The fast-track for this season has no crop decision protocol, and its wording is inverted. S10 says "Anything not irreversible continues as the incumbent manager planned." Seed, fertiliser and chemicals bought and fields planted are the irreversible acts. If read literally, they are held until G1 (wk 3-6) plus 4-8 weeks of lead time, and the window is missed in the S-temperate "planting now" and tropics cells. If read the other way, the incumbent commits the whole season's inputs and crop mix before the feed, water and labour models exist, and before T-tier controls, direct bank feeds or spot-counts are in place (G2). Why it matters: the first season's crop mix decides feed-vs-cash, labour peak, and cash exposure, and it is also the easiest window for input theft or padded orders. Fix: add a week-1 to week-2 "this-season memo". The agronomist or extension officer reviews the incumbent's crop plan against the window. The owner approves a capped input spend (and the feed-vs-cash split, defaulting to feed-first) under the T2/T3 rules from day 1. Receipt counts and application records are reconciled by someone other than the incumbent. State plainly that irreversible decisions inside the window are made once, with this memo, and not deferred to G1.

2. [MAJOR] Gate timeline vs season table vs staffing: the pilot and the new manager both land in peaks, contradicting the plan's own rule. G0 ends wk 3, G1 wk 6, the manager offer waits for G1, and recruitment takes 3-6 months. So a new manager starts about wk 19-32, i.e. roughly Mar-May 2027. G2 (wk 6-16) requires "manager and workers" to field-test SOPs, which is impossible if there is no manager until after it. G3 is "in season" for at least 8 weeks, so it runs about Feb-Apr 2027. That is the lambing/calving/planting surge in N-temperate and harvest/autumn breeding in S-temperate. This breaks "do not launch ... inside a peak" and puts an unproven manager into the peak with L1 controls only. Why it matters: a manager hand-over during birthing or planting is the classic failure point, and the plan never says who runs the farm between the incumbent's exit and the new manager's competence. Fix: (a) state that G2 field-tests use the incumbent or interim lead plus workers, and the new manager does a shadow period; (b) add a rule that a new manager's start, or G3 start, is moved out of a peak, or the incumbent stays through it (retention agreement); (c) for G3, name the peak as the intended stress test but with a retained consultant stockman/agronomist on call and the L1 approval regime.

3. [MAJOR] The drought/climate fix is thin: one lagging trigger and no crop-side risk. R1 asked for destocking at pasture or rainfall thresholds, irrigation sizing and crop insurance triggers. v2 has only "destock when feed-days remaining < 60". That equals the 2-month reserve, so the trigger fires the moment the reserve is touched. It is lagging, because the grass has already failed. It ignores execution lead time (buyer, transport, movement documents, 4-8 weeks), and distressed sales in a regional drought are at the price floor. The water model counts stock and poultry cooling but not crop or irrigation demand, and it has no rainfall/pasture-growth monitoring. Why it matters: drought is the dominant correlated loss for crops, pasture and water at once, and the plan will react at the worst price. Fix: add leading triggers. Examples: cumulative rainfall or pasture-growth shortfall against a season-point, with a pre-agreed staged response (stop buying breeding stock, early cull of the bottom class, early weaning, sell store animals) at amber, and full destock at red. Set the feed-days red above reserve-plus-lead-time (e.g. 90 days projected to season end). Add irrigation demand (mm/ha) to the water model if any area is irrigated (G0 answers this). Name crop/forage insurance as a G1 decision, not just a line in S5.

4. [MAJOR] Cross-enterprise feed and manure paths are not controlled; the plan relies on separation that on-farm feed production defeats. S6 separates poultry from ruminants (distance, staff, boots), but S4 plans on-farm grain/forage feeding all enterprises. Unaddressed: (a) shared mixer, store, silo, trailer or feed truck. Poultry rations commonly carry ionophore coccidiostats, which are toxic or lethal to cattle, sheep and goats (and to horses), and cross-contamination in a shared mill or bin is a classic cause of ruminant deaths. (b) Poultry litter and manure spread on grazing land: it is a Salmonella/HPAI/pathogen path, very high in N and P, and can carry enough copper to be a sheep hazard. There are no spreading-to-grazing intervals and no ban on feeding litter. (c) Medicated feed has no withdrawal tie to the "no-sale flag", which covers treatments only. Why it matters: it is a low-probability, high-loss event with a trivially cheap control, and it sits exactly where WP2, WP3, WP4 and WP5 meet, so nobody owns it. Fix: add a feed-flow rule to S6 and WP4. Dedicated or flushed-and-logged equipment for medicated poultry feed; labelled stores; poultry feed never to ruminants; no litter in ruminant feed; litter/manure spreading map with a grazing exclusion interval (vet/agronomist to set) and a species exclusion list (no litter on sheep pasture unless tested); all medicated and top-dressed feeds entered in the same register as medicines.

5. [MAJOR] G1 rests on agronomic baselines that cannot be measured in the time allowed, and the Farm Model has no owner or sign-off. G0 may exit with rows marked "unknown, baseline audit to resolve", and the audit is weeks 1-3. Soil lab turnaround alone is 2-4 weeks. Pasture production, 1-in-10 dry-month water yield and crop yields need a season or a dry-season pump test, and today's date may not be the right season for any of them. G1(b) requires net margin >= 0, but that margin may be computed on assumed yields, carrying capacity and borehole supply. Separately, S9 has no row for the Farm Model, rations or water supply: nobody signs the feed balance (no nutritionist), the water yield/abstraction view (no hydrogeologist or regulator), or the model as a whole. The integration reviewer is the only check, and it is an agent. WP0 is also circular: it is "built first", but its yield, labour-norm and mortality parameters come from WP1-WP5. Why it matters: the go/no-go and the destock rules are only as good as these inputs, and G1 is the plan's main decision. Fix: (a) tag every model input as measured / regional benchmark / assumed. G1 may pass only as "conditional" if any of yield, stocking capacity or water supply is "assumed", with a margin of safety and a re-confirmation trigger (first harvest, first dry season) before restocking or expansion; (b) add S9 rows: Farm Model (agronomist and accountant co-sign, owner approves), ration/feed balance (nutritionist or vet), water (hydrogeologist/permit holder); (c) name a model owner and version log, and say WP0 starts with regional defaults that WP outputs overwrite at G1.

## MINOR

6. [MINOR] G1(d) says feed, water and labour models "pass" with no criteria, against Principle 2 (measurable exit). Fix: define pass, e.g. feed deficit covered by budgeted purchases in every month of the base and drought scenarios; reliable supply >= demand in the 1-in-10 dry month with 24-48 h storage; peak-month labour <= ~85% of rostered hours after leave, with named casual sources for the remainder.

7. [MINOR] S7 has no crop, forage or feed signal. Feed-days remaining, grain moisture and bin temperature, rainfall deficit, pest threshold breach and irrigation failure are absent, and the 60-day destock trigger has no data owner or measurement method. Fix: add 3-4 rows (feed-days, stored-grain moisture/hot spot, rainfall/pasture deficit, scouting threshold) and keep the 8-12 KPI cap by retiring others.

8. [MINOR] Pesticide and chemical residues are tracked for people and poultry (no spraying near poultry or water) but not for animals and food. Missing: grazing and feeding intervals for treated pasture, forage and crop residues; treated-seed handling; and linking spray records to the same no-sale/withdrawal flag used for medicines. Fix: add to the approved-chemicals list a livestock-withdrawal column and the flag link.

9. [MINOR] Crops and forage are treated as one enterprise that G1 can cut. Forage is not optional for ruminants, and the land base competes between forage and cash crops. Fix: have the G1 screen split forage/feed (tied to the ruminant decision) from cash crops (a separate gross margin per labour-hour), so that "drop crops" cannot silently remove feed security.

10. [MINOR] Machinery and contractor capacity is absent. Planting, hay and harvest timeliness depends on machine capacity, breakdown cover and custom-hire booking; the labour histogram will look fine while hay is lost to a weather window. Fix: add a machinery/contractor line to the Farm Model and WP5 (capacity by operation per week, pre-booked contractors, spares), and include machinery cost in G1.

11. [MINOR] S10 columns are not actionable for everyone. S-temperate omits winter cereal sowing (Apr-Jun) and the tropics cell mixes regional terms ("Rabi/short-rains") with no bimodal vs unimodal split. Mediterranean (winter-wet), arid/irrigated and equatorial cases fit no column, and the Oct-Nov window is the highest-stakes one. Fix: add a fifth "other, build from local rainfall records" column with a rule that the agronomist produces the actual calendar at G0, plus the two missing cells.

12. [MINOR] The outbreak standstill (animals, manure, vehicles, people) conflicts with peak operations: harvest, hay, feed deliveries and shared labour. None of the five G2 tabletop scenarios tests an outbreak or a manager exit inside a peak. Fix: add one combined scenario (notifiable suspect during harvest or lambing) and name which essential feed and water deliveries are allowed under a vet-approved exemption.

13. [MINOR] Crop-side verification is thin. Yield and sales are the least auditable numbers on a crop farm. Fix: weighbridge or calibrated-trailer tickets at harvest, GPS field areas, yield cross-check with satellite or neighbour benchmarks in the unannounced audit, and buyer weigh-in reconciled to store out-turn (the latter exists for sales; extend it to harvest).

14. [MINOR] Capital for manure store, hermetic/grain storage and water backup is not named in G1 budgets or in the T3 capital list, although the plan requires all three. Fix: add them as G1 capital lines with a build-before-need date.

VERDICT: 5 MAJOR remaining
