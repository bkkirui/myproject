## MAJOR

1. [MAJOR] The "trough" that the manager start and G3 are moved into is not a trough under the plan's own season table, and the decision sheet misstates it.
   - Problem: The decision sheet says "Manager in post ~Apr-Jun 2027, in a trough." Section 3 defines troughs as N temperate Jun-Aug or Nov-Jan, and S temperate Jan-Feb or Jun-Jul. Section 9's table says N temperate Mar-May is calving/lambing/planting and Jun-Sep is "harvest, hay, annual count". So N Apr-May is a peak, and N Jun-Aug (hay and harvest) is a peak by the table. The only N trough consistent with both is Nov-Jan, which is November 2027 for a manager who is hired after G1. S temperate fails the same way. Apr-May is harvest and autumn breeding, and Jan-Feb is "hay, weaning" in the table but is called a trough in section 3. Only S Jun-Jul is clean. So in almost every cell the headline "in a trough" is false. The section 3 chain compounds this: G1+10 weeks is about April 2027 (G2 field tests), manager start is May-Aug, G2b adds 8 weeks and G3 adds 8 more, so G3 lands in Oct-Dec 2027. That is N tupping and S lambing/summer planting.
   - Why it matters: This is R2-A2 re-labelled instead of fixed. The owner is approving dates on a sheet that tells them the manager arrives in a quiet period. The real position is that the interim lead must carry a full peak (or two) after G1, which is unbudgeted (item 6) and has no stated term. The A3 date ("earliest Q3 2028") and the pre-G1 spend are both sensitive to this. If the N trough is really Nov 2027, A3 moves to Q4 2028 or later.
   - Fix: Make one table the source of truth and derive the trough from it. For each column, list peak months and trough months once (section 9), and have section 3 and the decision sheet refer to that table. Replace "Apr-Jun 2027, in a trough" with a hemisphere-conditional statement, for example "manager start N: Nov 2027 or, if earlier, with the interim lead retained through Aug; S: Jun-Jul 2027". State the interim lead's minimum term (through the first full peak after manager start plus handover) and restate the A3 earliest date from the corrected start.

2. [MAJOR] The this-season fast-track is declared but not operable, and it stops at 29 Oct.
   - Problem: (a) The five actions this month do not include engaging the agronomist or extension officer or naming the interim lead, yet the memo is due by 29 Oct, two weeks out, and depends on both. (b) The memo's "capped input spend" has no cap rule and no home. It is not in the pre-G1 spend list, so it is unclear whether it sits inside the "lesser of quote or 1.5% of revenue" cap. It is also unclear whether it counts toward the G1-signed cumulative-loss limit (section 3 says pre-G1 spend counts). (c) The controls that make a capped spend safe (T3 vendor creation, direct bank feeds, a non-incumbent reconciler) are "built" at G2, about April 2027 (Appendix B and section 1: nothing is built until G1). The memo says receipts are "reconciled by someone other than the incumbent" but does not name who, and no interim vendor-creation or payment rule applies. (d) The memo covers only the current window, and section 9 says "everything else continues as is." Concretely: N temperate "Dec-Feb: winter feeding, order seed" falls after the memo and before or around G1 (17 Dec to 29 Jan). The seed order for Mar-May planting is then either made by the incumbent with no model, or deferred until it misses lead time. S temperate Dec-Feb hay contracting and Nov-Mar dry-season stocking decisions fall in the same gap.
   - Why it matters: The first season's input purchases, winter feed and seed orders are the largest early-cash and easiest-theft decisions. The plan identified this exact window as the fraud-prone one (R2-A1) and then left it without owner, payee control or scope past 29 Oct.
   - Fix: (i) Add to the five actions "engage agronomist/extension for the memo; name the interim lead". (ii) State the memo cap as a rule (for example the lesser of a fixed share of last year's input spend and a figure the owner sets) and say explicitly whether it sits inside the pre-G1 cap and the loss limit. (iii) Add a short "interim controls" paragraph to section 9 that applies until G2. It should cover owner-only new payees, bank statements direct to the owner or accountant from G0, and a named reconciler (coordinator or accountant). (iv) Extend the memo into a rolling "window memo" refreshed at each season boundary until G2 (next: 1 Dec), with seed and winter-feed commitments pre-approved at a minimum or deferred by rule.

3. [MAJOR] Farm Model ownership and funding are still unassigned, so the G1 decision instrument has no builder, no date, and some of its required signers are not in the pre-G1 budget.
   - Problem: Section 4 requires "one named owner", and section 8 requires "model owner named", but the owner is not named by anyone at any date. The G0 exit criteria (section 3) list "lawyer engaged, coordinator named" and no model owner. Section 8 requires co-signs from the vet, agronomist and accountant, plus a nutritionist or vet for the ration and a hydrogeologist or permit holder for water. The pre-G1 spend list (decision sheet) has the baseline audit with vet review, lawyer, accountant, "agronomist memo", interim lead, coordinator and manager search. That is not a Farm Model co-sign for the agronomist, and there is no nutritionist or hydrogeologist. "WP outputs overwrite" regional defaults (section 4, A6), but v3 defines no work packages. The reader cannot tell who produces the outputs. The conditional pass is the intended escape, but it means G1 is more likely to be conditional by construction.
   - Why it matters: G1 is the programme's main decision and the owner is being asked to approve a capped spend to reach it. If the capped spend cannot buy the signers that the plan itself requires, either the cap is breached (returns to the owner) or the model is signed by agents or by unqualified people, contradicting section 8's "licensed humans decide".
   - Fix: Add "Farm Model owner named (a person, with hours and a fee)" to the G0 exit criteria. Add the agronomist model co-sign, nutritionist or vet ration review and the water view (hydrogeologist or permit holder, or a documented decision to accept a conditional G1 without it) to the quoted pre-G1 list. Either define who produces WP outputs in one line or remove the references. State in one sentence that a manager offer made on a conditional G1 is itself conditional or notice-bounded (see item 11).

## MINOR

4. [MINOR] G2 field tests land in the N-temperate lambing/planting peak, and the gate depends on workers' time. G1+10 weeks from 29 Jan is about mid-April, and the SOP tests need "the interim lead plus workers" (and tabletops with a live call). Fix: allow paper or tabletop tests of birthing and planting SOPs in the peak, with the field test of the same SOP deferred to the next trough. Say which SOPs are exempted.

5. [MINOR] Drought triggers are sound in content but have three execution gaps.
   - (a) Season-points are "agronomist-supplied" with no date. S temperate and many tropical dry seasons (Nov-Mar) arrive before G2, when A2 is built.
   - (b) Only the feed-days red row is "owner pre-authorises the staged response". The rainfall-row responses at amber and red (early cull, sell store animals, full destock) are T3 disposals under B1 and need the owner, who may be unreachable in a regional event.
   - (c) The 4-8 week lead time and the owner's 2 h representative window are not reconciled.
   - Fix: pre-authorise all staged rows in the G1-signed drought plan, with sale ceilings. Have the memo supply interim season-points and a feed-days count now.

6. [MINOR] The cost bands do not place the interim lead or the consultant agronomist. B10's numerator lists the consultant stockman (year 1) but not the interim lead (retention agreement or contract manager), and not the consultant agronomist that the A3 criterion keeps "for the whole first cycle". Management pay is excluded. The interim lead is neither. Under item 1 the interim lead may serve for most of a year. Fix: place both explicitly (management cost or oversight cost) and restate the band check.

7. [MINOR] The labour model does not say it includes control and compliance hours. The plan adds many tasks that peak at the same time as production work: tag photographs for each death, two-person load-out and gate pass with a withdrawal-check line, daily photo transmission, a euthanasia-authorised person per shift, night watch, hosting spot-counts, and a separate poultry crew. G1 item 3 tests the peak month at 85% of rostered hours, but A5 only says labour is "checked against rosters". Compulsory 10-day leave for the manager and bookkeeper has no rule against falling in a peak. Fix: add control and compliance hours to the labour histogram definition, and set leave windows to troughs in the charter.

8. [MINOR] Feed-flow and litter rules have no human owner. A4 is "owned by WP4", and v3 has no WP4. Section 8 gives signers (vet and agronomist) but not an operational owner. Fix: name the unit lead or the manager as owner of the dedicated-equipment, labelled-store and spreading-map controls.

9. [MINOR] The Day-7 pack covers notifiable response and safety but not the day-to-day poultry loss controls. If poultry exist, there is no interim daily mortality count, no numeric red (A2) and no generator or ventilation fallback until G2 or later. S temperate heat in Dec-Feb is the classic window for house failure (R1-5, R1-6). Fix: add "if poultry present: adopt the A2 broiler/layer red as an interim rule, daily count to the coordinator, generator test and curtain/ventilation fallback" to the pack.

10. [MINOR] Dates and owner hours do not reconcile. The decision sheet says 20-30 h "over ~8 weeks" while the plan date for G1 is 29 Jan (about 15 weeks). Year 1 is quoted at 400-450 h (about 8 h/week), but the phase rows (25 + 50 + pilot at 3-4 h/week + 70 h presence + A2 at 2-3 h/week) cannot reach that unless the pilot and A2 each run most of a year, and the section does not say what "year 1" starts from. The kill trigger (150% of the phase total) depends on these figures. Fix: state the year-1 window and recompute from the rows.

11. [MINOR] The conditional-G1 re-confirmation triggers (first harvest, first dry season, first birthing season) can fall after the manager offer and start. Recruitment is 3-6 months, notice is 90 days, and the first harvest in N temperate is Jun-Sep 2027. So the commitment is made before the assumptions are checked. Fix: make the offer contingent on the first re-confirmation, or limit it to a probation-length commitment, for conditional passes. This links to item 3.

12. [MINOR] The interim lead may be the incumbent, who is also a control subject. The intake asks about relatives among staff and suppliers, and the lead then tests SOPs that constrain the role and holds the retention agreement through peaks. Fix: state that the incumbent cannot be the interim lead if the baseline audit finds a related-party or unreconciled-stock issue, and name the contract-manager fallback and its lead time before G1.

VERDICT: 3 MAJOR remaining
