**Day-7 safety pack** (Appendix A0): vet-of-record, verified authority number, standstill cards, emergency letter, interim treatment list, withdrawal and live-season checks, interim poultry rules.
**Day-7 financial pack and interim regime** (Appendix B0): lawyer's short go, then count and access changes the same day; float and new-payee freeze; counts at ~6–8 weeks, at G1, at least quarterly (maximum 4 months) until the B6 regime starts at G2 exit, at any custody change and at handover, plus a monthly roll-forward on independent inputs; books-review tests; no sale or transfer of stock, produce, feed, machinery or fuel without your written OK (B0.4).

**G0 exit:** intake complete; audit and both packs done; lawyer engaged and incumbent handling cleared; coordinator and **Farm Model owner named, with hours and fee**; interim lead named (provisional); non-temperate season calendar; interim regime B0 in force; role sign-offs recorded.

## 4. G1: viability and scope
**Farm Model** (owner named at G0, version log, section 7 signers): herd/flock dynamics, feed balance, labour by month **including control hours**, water (incl. irrigation), machinery and contractors, 24-month cash flow. Inputs are tagged measured / benchmark / assumed; an assumed yield, carrying capacity or water supply gives only a **conditional pass** (15% safety margin; re-confirm at first harvest, dry season, birthing season), and a manager offer is then probation-length or contingent on re-confirmation.

**Pass criteria (all):**
1. **Hurdle (procedure in B12).** All figures real. Value each option you declare acceptable as **annual after-risk income to you on the same asset base**: OMBM (normalised for unpaid family labour) less management and control cost, your time at your hourly shadow price, post-G1 set-up or transition cost over 3–5 years (pre-G1 spend is sunk), and a risk charge (default 3–5%) on operating capital at risk. Hold options keep the land, so they include its return **and g × land value**; sale is net proceeds of land, stock and machinery at your declared real return, and counts only if you would sell. **Drop any option with value at or below zero or failing the stress limit** (default: worst-scenario net cash flow no lower than −10% of capital at risk). Rank the rest. **Self-run first: proceed. A hold option first: restructure. Sale first: conditional exit (independent valuation and tax advice first). None left: no viable option.** The output prints the g at which sale and the best hold option swap places. A winner not executable at quoted terms yields to the next-ranked; a self-run or management-company winner under 10% of OMBM is a conditional pass.
2. **Control cost** fits the Appendix B §10 band cap **and** is at most one-third of OMBM; the core set is never dropped (if it does not fit, scale down or lease). Optional controls pass an expected-loss test.
3. **Feed:** every month of base and drought cases covered by budgeted purchases. **Water:** supply >= demand in the 1-in-10 driest month, plus 72 h storage (default). **Labour:** peak month <=85% (default) of rostered hours after leave and control tasks.
4. **Enterprises:** rank by gross margin per labour-hour and supervision need; default 2–3 in year 1; forage screened separately from cash crops; poultry needs positive justification; surplus sold down.
5. **Capital lines** with build-before-need dates; **vet access** (poultry-competent vet or lab route); **insurance** and drought plan decided.

## 8. Window memo (weeks 1–2, then rolling)
Irreversible acts in the current window (seed, fertiliser, chemicals, planting, restocking, long contracts, winter feed, next-window seed orders) are decided **once per window** in a memo, not deferred to G1 or left to the incumbent unreviewed. The agronomist reviews the plan against the local window and supplies season-points and a feed-days count; lead-time commitments default to the agronomist's minimum quantity, topped up after G1. **Cap** (inputs, stock purchases and sales; tupping, weaning, sale timing): the lesser of your figure, last year's audited spend and the agronomist's quantity; it is operating budget outside the pre-G1 cap; shrinkage found counts to the loss limit. The agronomist declares conflicts; the memo carries staged drought rows. Controls until G2: B0. Refreshed each season boundary (next 1 Dec).
