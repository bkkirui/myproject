# Farm Autonomy Plan — Draft v1 (for owner approval)

Farm: Livestock (cattle, sheep, goats), Poultry, Crops. Owner: leading academic; goal = farm operates without owner's daily involvement.

## 1. Design principle: management by exception
Owner does no daily work/decisions. Farm runs on written procedures, small paid team, automated monitoring. Owner sees only:
- Weekly one-page dashboard (KPIs, cash, open issues)
- Exception alerts when thresholds breached (mortality spike, water failure, cash below floor, disease signal)
- Monthly/quarterly decisions: capital spend, hire/fire, marketing & enterprise-mix changes
Everything else delegated against written authority limits.

## 2. Structure
- Owner: board-level; approves budget, reviews dashboard (~1 hr/week)
- Farm Manager (key hire): runs daily ops, delegated authority up to a spending limit; single point of dependence -> deputy + documented handover
- Unit leads: Livestock (cattle, small ruminants), Poultry, Crops (may be combined by scale)
- Workers: herders, poultry attendants, field hands; run from daily task sheets
- External retainers: large-animal vet, agronomist, accountant/bookkeeper, security; scheduled visits + on-call
- Independent monthly audit (external/rotating peer): stock counts, feed & drug use vs records, cash.

## 3. Work packages for specialist agents
1. Livestock Production (cattle, sheep, goats): grazing/rotation, rations by class, stocking rates; breeding calendar (AI/bull), calving/lambing/kidding, weaning; health program (vaccination, deworming/FAMACHA, hoof care, quarantine SOP, biosecurity); animal ID & records (weights, BCS).
2. Poultry: flock cycle plan (layers or broilers), daily checks, vaccination schedule, feed/water, lighting/ventilation, egg/bird handling; mortality thresholds, disease & avian influenza biosecurity, cleanout/restocking SOP.
3. Crop: crop calendar & rotation, soil testing & fertility, seed/input procurement, IPM, irrigation scheduling, harvest/drying/storage SOPs, weather contingency playbook.
4. Animal Health & Biosecurity (cross-cutting): disease response/emergency playbook, withdrawal-period tracking, vet-call criteria, mortality disposal.
5. Operations & Infrastructure: preventive maintenance (water, power, fencing, vehicles, machinery), spare parts stock, security plan, "what to do if X breaks".
6. Finance & Controls: chart of accounts, budgets by enterprise, petty-cash & purchase-approval matrix, monthly reconciliation, cash-floor triggers, insurance, tax/compliance calendar.
7. Supply Chain & Marketing: input procurement/preferred suppliers, sales channels & contracts, pricing rules, reorder points for feed/drugs/seed.
8. People & Management: job descriptions, rosters, training, KPI-linked incentives, disciplinary/escalation ladder, Farm Manager delegated-authority charter.
9. Data & Monitoring: phone-form record keeping into central spreadsheet/database; weekly dashboard & automated alerts; optional sensors/cameras (water tank levels, cold chain, gate cameras); Stata/R analysis templates.
10. Risk & Compliance: risk register, licensing/regulatory, animal welfare & food safety, succession & continuity plan.
Integration reviewer: cross-checks feed demand vs crop output, labor rosters vs tasks, cash flow vs procurement; compiles master Operations Manual.

## 4. Deliverables
Master Operations Manual (SOPs as checklists); daily/weekly/monthly/seasonal calendars & role task sheets; delegation-of-authority matrix & escalation tree with "call the owner" triggers; KPI set & dashboard spec with alert thresholds; budget & cash-flow model; monthly audit protocol; 90-day transition plan.

## 5. Phasing
- Phase 0 (wk1): owner answers questions; agents briefed
- Phase 1 (wk2-4): current-state assessment, risk register, Farm Manager charter
- Phase 2 (wk4-8): enterprise SOPs, finance controls, monitoring design
- Phase 3 (wk8-12): integration review, pilot with Farm Manager, revisions
- Phase 4 (months 3-6): shadow period (owner = escalation point only), then hands-off review

## 6. Open questions to owner
Location/country; scale (head counts, hectares, crops, poultry type); current staffing/manager; infrastructure (water, power, connectivity); markets; budget; output format (Word/Excel/doc; records in Excel/Sheets).
