## 1. Principles
Management by exception, not absence. Autonomy is earned by gates, not calendar. Economics and scope before SOPs: the appendices are design defaults, not for approval now. Safety acts first and notifies in parallel. Any one person, including the representative, can be wrong, absent or compromised.

## 4. G1: viability and scope
**Farm Model** (owner named at G0, version log, section 7 signers): herd/flock dynamics, monthly feed balance, labour by month **including control and compliance hours**, water (incl. irrigation), machinery and contractors, 24-month cash flow. Starts on regional defaults; each input is tagged measured / benchmark / assumed. Any assumed yield, carrying capacity or water supply gives only a **conditional pass** (15% margin of safety; re-confirm at first harvest, dry season, birthing season); a manager offer then is probation-length or contingent on the first re-confirmation.

**Pass criteria (all):**
1. **Hurdle:** base-case net margin after management and control cost, **less your time at your hourly shadow price** (you declare it) and **less one-off set-up costs amortised over 3–5 years** (entity, legal, SOPs, tools, interim lead, consultant backstop, pre-G1 spend, ramp-up), exceeds the best alternative's net income (lease, contract, sale; like for like, including its owner hours) plus a risk premium (default 3–5% of **operating capital at risk = stock, machinery, working capital**; land is charged only against a sale alternative, since you keep it either way). Set-up items already in B10 are counted once. Worked example: B12. Stress losses stay within the signed limit: price and yield −20%; drought −30%; disease −10% stock; inputs +25%; **notifiable event** (largest enterprise; restriction length and compensation per authority; poultry default: full loss, no sales 90 days, 6–12 month lag).
2. **Control cost** fits the Appendix B §10 band cap **and** is at most one-third of pre-management gross margin; the core set is never dropped (if it does not fit, scale down or lease). Optional controls pass an expected-loss test.
3. **Feed:** every month of base and drought cases covered by budgeted purchases. **Water:** supply >= demand in the 1-in-10 driest month, plus 72 h storage (default). **Labour:** peak month <=85% (default) of rostered hours after leave and control tasks.
4. **Enterprises:** rank by gross margin per labour-hour and supervision need; default 2–3 in year 1; forage screened separately from cash crops; poultry needs positive justification; surplus sold down on a season-timed plan.
5. **Capital lines** with build-before-need dates; **vet access** adequate, including a poultry-competent vet or lab route; **insurance** and drought plan (staged responses pre-authorised with sale ceilings) decided.

## 8. Window memo (weeks 1–2, then rolling)
Irreversible acts in the current window (seed, fertiliser, chemicals, planting, restocking, long contracts, winter feed, next-window seed orders) are decided **once per window** in a memo, not deferred to G1 or left to the incumbent's unreviewed plan. The agronomist reviews the plan against the local window and supplies season-points and a feed-days count; lead-time commitments default to the agronomist's minimum quantity, topped up after G1. **Cap** (inputs, stock purchases and sales; tupping, weaning, sale timing): the lesser of your figure, last year's audited spend and the agronomist's quantity; it is operating budget outside the pre-G1 cap; shrinkage found counts toward the loss limit. The agronomist signs a conflict declaration (no retailer links); the memo carries interim staged drought rows with sale ceilings. Controls until G2: B0. Refreshed at each season boundary (next 1 Dec). Feed-first.
